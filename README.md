# Parallel Emerald Trainer Sheet

Pokémon Parallel Emerald v1.2 trainer teams on Hard / Against the Odds difficulty (gym leaders, rivals, Aqua/Magma,
route bosses, other bosses, the Elite Six and every normal trainer), laid out like the Null trainer sheet: Astral dark
theme with a light mode toggle, held-item icons and colour-coded EV spreads. Tag battles show your partner's team right
under the enemy team.

Live: https://owakeningowl.github.io/parallel-trainer-sheet/

Data from the community "Trainer and Boss Battles - Parallel Emerald v1.2" spreadsheet (Hard & Against the Odds
columns). EVs apply on Hard only; Against the Odds trainers have none.
